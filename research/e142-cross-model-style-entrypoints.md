**Superseded product direction / owner evidence:** [Issue #33 / T11](e142-art-host-gates.md) makes ART the lead and records the owner's confirmed inability to invoke dedicated-gallery Add Effects through normal P/A/S/M Picture Mode. Do not re-propose this report's historical B→A→B audit. The observation is a workflow constraint, not byte-level state/renderer separation. **Decision: continue investigating A as the daily selector and B as the constrained ART editor; prioritize a separate shooting-bank/recall mechanism for complete looks. Pause E-P7 implementation.** Four exact-model manuals support shared *interaction concepts*, with important differences. ## Evidence and the convergence hypothesis

- **D — exact-model official:** inspected manufacturer manuals/pages; verified as documentary evidence, not this researcher's camera tests. E-M5 II and PEN-F PDFs include version-specific addenda; a feature added by an update is not assigned to an earlier version.
- **O — E-P7 owner-observed:** [Issue #31](https://github.com/ZakuZakuu/ep7-film-hack/issues/31) records normal P/A/S/M→OK→LV super control panel→Picture Mode (**A**), and mode dial ART→dedicated filter/effect editor (**B**). Some B controls are unavailable in A. WB/WB shift and Highlight & Shadow remain global in the owner's switching observations, including the profile lever. No capture, firmware version read, transport trace or memory observation accompanied that report.
- **V — verified offline bytes:** privately held E-P7, owner-reported **1.1**, original **111017984 bytes / SHA-256 `9d1610124ad98f6edae4540182205f06c9c703d9033ef0c4f19847193f95eaeb`**; decoded OE #0 **26214336 bytes / SHA-256 `5dd9af164b4590c7e82f6905c919b3cc450bb58f2d5742ab2a0e3a4eb2e26190`**. Original/OE/decoded guards passed before/after. No firmware was downloaded this round.
- **H / U — hypothesized / unknown:** interpretations and missing observations. Similar labels, menus or TruePic branding never establish binary, address, ABI or patch compatibility.

**H0, falsifiable:** an extra selected in E-P7 B survives selection of the *same base and variant* through A and is consumed by native shooting. Expected observation: B-selected effect remains in A output and on returning to B, while independently recorded global settings stay controlled. Counter-observation: selection clears it or separate values are retained. The latter alone cannot distinguish separate storage from reset-on-selection. **Current result: H0 remains unverified.** The manual's statement that ART Picture Modes use ART settings supports investigation, but neither owner UI differences nor the parameter structure proves convergence.

**Resolve the scope, not the disagreement:** E-P7 manual pp49/53 explicitly says profile-editor tone changes save to the selected MONO/COLOR slot; O reports global tone across the lever workflow. Different editing contexts, internal bank switching versus lever switching, or a documentation/runtime difference are possibilities, **not findings**. Preserve both observations. Ordinary Picture Mode selection and profile-lever movement must not be promised to restore WB/shift/tone atomically. O does not independently test every profile-bank transition. This qualifies T9's documentary joint-storage claims; it does not erase the official wording.

**Bank-layer candidate:** Myset/Custom is a demonstrated original-camera mechanism for broader configuration recall, separate from Picture Mode per-base storage. OM-5 II supplies an actual naming precedent; do not claim named banks on the other three models. A bank saving a Picture Mode *selector* does **not** necessarily snapshot the shared per-filter adjustment table. Even documented C/Myset therefore cannot yet promise two independent saved versions of the same ART. The user-facing concept can transfer; supported fields, hardware gating, reset/recall behavior and OEM transfer eligibility require per-model adapters and documentation. No universal file/ABI adapter was implemented.

A smaller stock convenience also exists on E-M5 II (p102), PEN-F (p107) and [OM-5 II](https://learning.omsystem.com/OM-5MarkII/zz_html_manual/en/picture_mode_settings_131.html): hide unwanted Picture Mode choices through Picture Mode Settings. This reduces selector clutter, not creates/renames looks or recalls globals. That menu was **not established for E-P7** in the reviewed exact-model manual; no shortcut is borrowed from those models.

### Anchor 2 — selector-name metadata, rejected as an editor hook

Revalidated only the known SettingController name group **`[0x21fa74,0x21fb58)`**, 57 text pointers: selected method44 resolves to **`SettingController::changePicMode`**, identity **0x01022c**. The existing resolver **`[0x75068f,0x7506c4)`** returns text, not a handler. Existing candidate contexts around **0x786f46** and **0x79e80c** decode as unrelated immediate258 instructions; they are still **false identities**. No new candidate scan followed. There is no independently validated editor/selector implementation to trace. **Stop this branch.** T7's `saveCustom` indices and descriptor IDs53…56 do not remedy missing UI availability, ART binding or persistence semantics.

Together these anchors establish richer parameter organization and a firm negative control, **not A/B convergence**. An editor implementation address, its source-record writer and global-state transaction boundary are still missing. OI.Share strings, MakerNote structure presence and generic task requests cannot substitute for these.

## Highest-value next experiment; stop instead of another catalogue

**First: one separately authorized stock-only E-P7 B→A→B carryover audit.** Start with the manual's illustrated **Pop Art I + Frame**, change only that extra. Record base/variant, WB, A–B/G–M and all tone regions. Move from B to P/A/S/M and choose exactly the same base/variant in A, then return to B. If separately chosen, obtain matched original RAW+JPEG pairs with controlled exposure/WB and compare a narrow ExifTool allowlist (`ArtFilter`, `ArtFilterEffect`, relevant PictureMode/tone/WB fields) against GUI and JPEG evidence. Unknown tags remain unknown; private samples stay private.

Positive retention/output would support using B as an editor for an A-selected stock look; it would **not** identify the shared binary record or prove effect-equivalent preview. Lost effects would stop the simple B-edit/A-select route until reset-versus-separate-state is distinguished. No capture or physical operation was performed or authorized here.
